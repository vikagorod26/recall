05 · Components

Swipe card

The tip deck on the main screen. React component src/components/SwipeDeck.jsx, motion values from the --swipe-*, --deck-* and --duration-* tokens.

States

Live example · main screen layout

Swipe the top card left or right with a finger or the mouse. Arrow keys work on a focused card. Tips come from content.json, all categories, shuffled, no repeats until all 160 were shown.

Usage rules

  • Size: the deck sits in a .deck-area on a screen with --gutter-deck margins. The card keeps --card-aspect (width / height 0.78) and is centered in any spare height. Cards behind are offset and rotated by --deck-1-* / --deck-2-* (about 12px of overhang). Under 375px wide the title drops to --text-tip-narrow (34px) with tighter padding.
  • Content: category tag (category color + icon), title in Sora, subtext in Inter. Glassy dark card, 2 cards visible behind it.
  • Tip of the day is its own block under the deck. Never inside a card.
  • Both directions = next tip. No like/dislike, no labels or icons while dragging, no green/red.
  • Drag glow uses the card's category color, same for left and right, and grows with the drag (--swipe-glow-*).
  • Next card: cards are see-through glass (--color-surface-glass, 50%). Cards waiting behind show only their edge; the next one fades its content in as the top card is dragged, and back out if the drag snaps back. At rest the top card sits on a solid backing (--swipe-card-rest-solid) so its text stays readable; the backing fades out with the drag, reaching full see-through at the swipe threshold.
  • Swipe counts past --swipe-threshold of the card width, or on a fast flick. Shorter drags snap back.
  • Infinite deck: only the categories picked in onboarding, random order, no repeats until every tip was shown, never runs out.
  • Keys: cards are keyed by tip id, never by array index.
  • Mobile: card fills most of the screen inside the 28px --gutter-deck; the page doesn't scroll or bounce while swiping (lockScroll); short vibration on a successful swipe where supported.
  • Reduce motion: no tilt, no fly-out or shift animation; the next tip just appears.

Selection chips (small)

Compact toggle chips for lists and filters: the Settings category picker and the filters on the tips review page. Selected = brand outline, soft fill, check icon. Tap to toggle. Onboarding and the "why play?" sheet use the big cloud chips instead.

Splash

Shown on every launch: only the "Recall." wordmark (Sora, --text-splash), centered on the app background (--color-bg, same as the first HTML frame and the future PWA launch screen, so there's no white flash). Fades and glows in over --duration-splash-in, stays --duration-splash-first on first launch (then onboarding) or --duration-splash-return later (then the main screen), fades out over --duration-splash-out on top of the next screen. Tap (or Enter / Space / Esc) skips it. Reduce motion: plain fade, no scaling or growing glow. Splash.jsx

Tip of the day · border beam

"Tip of the day" sits under the deck with a thin beam of light travelling around its rounded border: brand purple at the head (--beam-head), fading into the lighter accent at the tail (--beam-tail). Subtle on purpose: a --beam-width line at --beam-opacity, a soft glow (--beam-glow-*), one loop every --duration-beam-loop, covering --beam-length of the loop. It draws the eye without competing with the deck. The loop follows the clock, so a new tip (or coming back from settings) never restarts it. Pauses when the app is in the background. Reduce motion: static soft purple border (--beam-static-opacity). Beam.jsx

Reduce motion

Reuse on another element

Put <Beam /> inside any position: relative element and set --beam-radius to its border radius (and --beam-inset to its border width, if it has one). The ring is masked to the radius, so it follows the corners exactly and is never clipped (the element must not use overflow: hidden if the glow should show outside it).

Screen transition · gather into the deck

Used from the category picker to the main screen; reuse it for any change that lands on the deck (for example settings back to the main screen). Under one second in total; the background stays --color-bg throughout.

  • Out (about 0.45s): headline, back button and Continue fade over --duration-gather-fade; unselected chips fade and shrink slightly; selected chips fly to the deck spot one after another (--gather-stagger), shrink to --gather-scale, glow in their category color (--gather-glow) and dissolve, over --duration-gather with --ease-gather.
  • In (about 0.45s): the top card rises --rise-distance on a gentle spring (--spring-rise-*), the cards behind fan out after it --rise-stagger apart, and the header, reason row, hint and "Tip of the day" fade in a beat later (--duration-screen-in). --duration-rise-settle marks the end; the "why play?" sheet waits for it.
  • Reduce motion: plain crossfade, nothing moves.
  • Code: ChipCloud gatherTo for the out part, SwipeDeck rise plus .app--enter for the in part.

Live · gravity on open

Chips fall from --chip-drop-distance above in random order, --chip-drop-stagger apart, fading in over --chip-drop-fade, and settle softly with one small overshoot (--spring-drop-*, damping ratio about 0.76), all within about 0.65s. Some chips get a random tilt up to --chip-tilt-max (--chip-tilt-share of them). Reduce motion: a simple fade (--duration-chip-fade). Tap chips to select.

Bottom sheet

Reusable sheet over the current screen (BottomSheet.jsx). Slides up on a spring (--spring-sheet-*) over a dimmed, slightly blurred backdrop (--sheet-backdrop*), so the screen stays visible behind it. Glassy dark surface like the tip cards, rounded top corners (--radius-sheet), grab handle. Closes by dragging down past --sheet-close-threshold of its height (or a flick), tapping the backdrop, Escape, or the phone's back button (it adds a history entry, so back closes the sheet, not the app). While open, everything behind it is inert: no taps, no swipes. Reduce motion: plain fade (--duration-sheet-fade). Optional footer: a pinned primary action that never scrolls away. onExited fires when the closing spring has finished, so the screen behind changes only after the sheet is gone.

Confirm button (footer)

Primary button "Show me tips", full width. Disabled until a reason is selected. Tapping it closes the sheet with the same spring it opened with and applies the reason; swipe down, backdrop and back close it as a skip.

Reason chips · brand tone

The category cloud chip with tone="brand", single-select (radio semantics). Brand purple instead of a category color, white text when selected (dark text on brand purple would only be 4.2:1). Same fall-in, press and checkmark animations. Tapping a chip only selects it (tap another to change your mind); the earlier answer starts preselected.

Reason pill & reaction line

Pill button above the deck (.pill-btn, 36px tall, 44px tap target): "Why play?" when no reason is set, the reason in brand style when it is. Tapping it opens the reason sheet. The reaction line (.reaction, Inter 400, 15px, muted text) appears under it once a reason is picked: a quiet aside, so the tip card stays the main thing on screen.

Category picker

Settings (onboarding uses the category cloud). Small chips in two even columns: category icon in its color, label, check when selected. Order follows content.json. In settings (keepOne) the last selected chip can't be turned off. CategoryPicker.jsx

Icon button & screen header

44px round icon button, always with an accessible label. Screen header = back button, centered title (gets focus when the screen opens), spacer. Buttons.jsx, ScreenHeader.jsx

Two-step button

For destructive actions like resetting the app. First tap arms it (brand outline + soft fill, new label), second tap within 4 seconds runs it, otherwise it disarms.
