# Backlog

Open questions and ideas for future updates of Recall. Nothing here is decided or scheduled yet. When one gets picked up, follow CLAUDE.md (styleguide first, tokens only, tone from research.md), then remove or update the entry here.

## Open questions

- **Similar tips.** Some tips start alike, for example "Watch a pro match highlight reel" / "Watch a pro play your champ", and the two "Read 10 pages…" tips. They aren't duplicates, but consider rewording so they feel more distinct.
- **Tips of the day are running out.** Most expire in November 2026, and after Jan 20, 2027 the "Tip of the day" block disappears. Needs a fresh check of current League events (Worlds, patches, events, skins) and new tips with `expires` dates.
  - Idea: update tips of the day automatically, for example a weekly scheduled check of League news that drafts new ones for review.

## Feature ideas

- **"Hold to Recall":** hold the card to fill a recall ring, then it turns into a 27-minute timer for that tip.
- **Celebration animation** when a timer is completed.
- **Category background glow:** the screen background glows in the current card's category color.
- **Easter-egg cards:** special cards, for example after 10 swipes.
- **Tilt shine:** a glass shine on the card that reacts to tilting the phone.
- **Shake to reroll** the deck.
- **Card flip on tap** with a funny stat on the back.
- **Panic button "I'M ALREADY IN QUEUE"** with a 27-second countdown (research.md §9).
- **Late-night mode:** sleep-focused tips after midnight (research.md §9).
- **"Lost 2 in a row?" mode** with stronger stop-tips (research.md §9).
- **"Games dodged" counter and parody achievements** (research.md §9).
- **Native Android version.** To decide: bundle the files in the app, or load the GitHub Pages site. A home-screen widget and notifications would be the main reasons to go native.
