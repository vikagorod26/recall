# Audience Research: League of Legends Players

Research brief for the "instead of queueing" app. Use it as the source of truth for tone, content and feature ideas.
Compiled: October 25, 2026.

---

## 1. Who the user is

- A League of Legends player who **loves and hates the game at the same time**. Self-aware about playing too much, jokes about it, but doesn't want a lecture.
- Love/hate is the community's default mood: players openly say they hate the game while playing it, yet it remains their favorite. Many players even tell newcomers "don't play League".
- Strong sense of identity: rank, main role, main champion, "my story" in the game (I was higher last season, I'm only here because of trolls, my champ got nerfed).
- Plays solo queue and with friends. The social side (friends calling you into a game) is a big reason to queue.
- Riot itself describes League as cyclical: people step away because of life, then come back. **The app is not about quitting forever. It's about not playing the next game on autopilot.**

## 2. Why they keep playing (the hooks)

| Hook | What it looks like | What the app can say |
|---|---|---|
| **"Just one more game"** | Always one more, almost always regretted | Interrupt the autopilot with a concrete, short alternative |
| **"Can't end on a loss"** | Loss aversion: keep queueing until a win | Make stopping after a loss feel like the smart, "high elo" move |
| **Tilt** | Frustration builds game after game; mostly caused by teammates, and players direct the worst of it at themselves | Physical reset activities: walk, shower, food, sleep |
| **~50% win rate rollercoaster** | Matchmaking keeps you near 50%, wins and losses come unpredictably | Joke that LP will be exactly where you left it |
| **Endless skill ceiling / rank chase** | "I deserve a higher rank", climbing never ends | "Climb" something else: pages, push-ups, a recipe |
| **Sunk cost** | Years and money already invested | Light jokes about time/LP, never guilt |
| **FOMO: events, skins, missions** | Limited-time events, missions, exclusive skins and icons | "Tip of the day" that defuses current FOMO |
| **Friends** | "Queue up?" message | Suggest doing something else *with* the same friends |

## 3. Pain points (what they complain about)

- **Toxicity**: flame, blame, chat abuse. One bad teammate can ruin the evening.
- **AFK / inting / trolls / people giving up**.
- **Autofill**: getting forced into a role you don't play.
- **Losing streaks** and feeling stuck in elo.
- **Sleep and lost days**: playing all night, waking up at 1 PM, feeling angry for no reason.
- **Burnout**: the game stops being fun but you keep clicking "Find Match".
- **Patch whiplash**: "the game changes too much to keep up" (Riot admitted 2025 had too many changes).

## 4. Key numbers to use in copy

- **Average game ≈ 27 minutes.** Use as a unit: "1 game = 27 min".
- After a loss, taking a break matters more than "one more to get back in form" (tilt research).
- 2 losses in a row is a popular community "stop rule".

## 5. Tone of voice

**The app is a teammate, not a therapist.**

DO:
- Speak like a player: casual, short, self-ironic.
- Use League slang naturally (glossary below).
- Joke *with* the user, not *at* the user.
- Keep tips concrete and doable in ~27 minutes or less.
- Assume the user will play again at some point. That's fine.

DON'T:
- Moralize, diagnose or use clinical "addiction" language in the UI.
- Shame ("you're wasting your life").
- Mock specific champions' players in a mean way or punch at real pro players.
- Use Riot logos, champion art, splash art, the Riot/LoL wordmark or official UI. Mentioning events and the game by name in text is fine; copying the look is not.

**Example lines (tone reference):**
- "Touch grass. Literally. 27 minutes, same as a game, zero LP lost."
- "You won't 'end on a win.' You'll end at 4am."
- "You farm minions all day. Farm some pancakes instead."
- "One episode ≈ one game. Nobody types 'jg diff' at you."
- "30 pages. No autofill: you pick your role."
- "Your LP will still be there tomorrow. So will the Rift."
- "Dodge this queue. No LP penalty in real life."
- "Tilted? /mute all works on the whole world for 20 minutes. It's called a walk."

## 6. Slang glossary

| Term | Meaning | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| queue / q up | Start searching for a game | "Don't queue. Cook." |
| one more game | The classic trap | Core joke of the app |
| tilt / tilted | Emotional frustration | Reset tips |
| LP | League Points (ranked) | "Zero LP lost" |
| elo / climb / hardstuck | Rank, ranking up, being stuck | "Climb the stairs instead" |
| autofill | Forced into a random role | "No autofill in real life" |
| dodge | Leave champ select (small penalty) | "Dodge this game" |
| ff / ff15 | Surrender vote (at 15 min) | "ff15 on doomscrolling" |
| gg / gg go next | Good game / move on | Closing lines |
| int / inting | Feeding on purpose | Light jokes |
| diff (jg diff) | "Your X player was worse" | Jokes about blame |
| main / OTP | Main champ, one-trick player | "Main a new hobby" |
| /mute all | Mute everyone in chat | Calm-down tips |
| farm / CS | Killing minions for gold | Food/productivity jokes |
| buff / nerf | Make stronger/weaker | "Sleep is the biggest buff" |
| patch notes | Update notes | "Life patch notes" feature |
| Rift | Summoner's Rift, main map | "The Rift will wait" |
| ARAM | Casual one-lane mode | "Even ARAM can wait" |
| pentakill | Killing all 5 enemies | Achievements |
| touch grass | Internet meme: go outside | Outdoors category, maybe app name |

## 7. Current context (for "tip of the day")

These expire. Re-check before each content update.

- **Worlds 2026**: October 15 to November 14 in the US. Swiss Stage runs Oct 23–31 in Allen, Texas; knockouts Nov 3–8; **final at Barclays Center, Brooklyn, Nov 14** (first East Coast Worlds final). T1 is chasing a historic fourth straight title.
- **Patch 26.21** (live since Oct 21): new **True Damage "Know My Name" skins**; **ARAM: Mayhem "Journey to Stardom"** event with band-themed Headliners (K/DA, Pentakill, HEARTSTEEL, True Damage) and mission-based progression; four champions added to League Classic.
- Events and missions = FOMO. Good angle: "The missions will still be there tomorrow. Tonight won't."

**Example tips of the day:**
- "Worlds is for watching, not for queueing. Nobody will remember your 2am game."
- "Journey to Stardom missions don't expire tonight. Your sleep does."
- "Your friends won't unfriend you for skipping one event."
- "The new skins aren't going anywhere. Your evening is."
- "Watch the Swiss Stage with friends instead of soloqueueing through it."

## 8. Categories (MVP)

1. Sports / workout
2. Movies & series
3. Reading
4. Outdoors (touch grass)
5. Cooking & food
6. Sleep & rest
7. Music
8. Friends & hanging out (incl. the same friends who invite you to queue)

Guidance: each tip should be specific ("20 push-ups", "one episode", "30 pages"), take ≤ 27 minutes, and include a light League reference in about half of the tips (not all, to avoid feeling forced).

## 9. Feature ideas grounded in the research

| Feature | Based on |
|---|---|
| "Why do you want to play?" poll: tilted / bored / friends are calling / event FOMO / habit; tips tailored to the reason | Different hooks need different responses |
| Panic button "I'M ALREADY IN QUEUE" with a 27-second countdown | "Just one more game" autopilot |
| "Lost 2 in a row?" mode with stronger stop-tips | Loss aversion, 2-loss stop rule |
| "Games dodged" counter: time saved (1 game ≈ 27 min) | Players think in games, not hours |
| Parody achievements & ranks ("Pentakill: 5 evenings without ranked", "Iron → Challenger of real life") | Rank/progression is a core motivator |
| "Life patch notes": weekly funny update with buffs/nerfs to the user's habits | Patch culture |
| Late-night mode (after midnight): sleep-focused tips | Sleep complaints |
| Auto-updated tips of the day from current events | FOMO from events/skins |
