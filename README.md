# Recall

**Less queueing. More living.**

A tiny app for those "one more game" nights. Instead of queueing up again, swipe for something to do. Each tip takes about a game's worth of time. Zero LP lost.

👉 **[Open Recall](https://vikagorod26.github.io/recall/)**

Nothing to sign up for, and nothing leaves your phone.

---

## Add it to your home screen

Add Recall to your home screen so it opens like a real app.

### iPhone (use Safari)

1. Open the link in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
4. Open **Recall** from your home screen.

### Android (use Chrome)

1. Open the link in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu (top right).
3. Tap **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**), then **Install**.
4. Open **Recall** from your home screen or app drawer.

---

## How it works

1. **Pick your interests:** sports, movies, reading, outdoors, cooking, sleep, music or friends.
2. **Get a tip:** a card with something to do instead of another game.
3. **Swipe** for the next one if it's not your vibe.
