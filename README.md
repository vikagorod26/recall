**Recall**

https://vikagorod26.github.io/recall/
It’s for those “one more game” nights: swipe for something to do instead. Nothing to sign up for, and nothing leaves your phone.

Add it to your home screen so it opens like a real app:

iPhone (use Safari):

Open the link in Safari.
Tap the Share button (square with an arrow pointing up).
Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
Open Recall from your home screen.

Android (use Chrome):

Open the link in Chrome.
Tap the ⋮ menu (top right).
Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app), then Install.
Open Recall from your home screen or app drawer.
